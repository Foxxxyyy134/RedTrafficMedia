Questions

Before you apply.

Doesn't a percentage of spend reward you for spending more?

It's the fair question to ask. That's why our targets are set on new-customer CAC and contribution margin, agreed with you before we start. If more spend stops being profitable, we recommend pulling it back, and we put that in writing in the monthly review.

Why is the minimum $100k a month?

Below that level, platforms don't get enough conversions to learn quickly, and a full senior team costs too much as a share of your budget. You'd get better value from a freelancer or a smaller agency until you reach that scale.

Who owns the ad accounts and creative?

You do. We work inside your Business Manager and ad accounts, and every creative file we produce belongs to you, including raw footage from creators.

Is creative production really included?

Yes. Strategy, scripting, editing, design and creator sourcing are part of the fee. Creator usage fees and large shoots are passed through at cost and approved by you first.

What do you need from our team?

Admin access to ad accounts and analytics, a weekly 30-minute call, quick sign-off on creative, and someone who can ship landing page and offer changes within a few days.

How long is the contract?

A 90-day initial term, which is how long a proper rebuild takes to show up in the numbers. After that it's rolling with 30 days' notice.
